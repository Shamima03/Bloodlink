Privacy Policy

BloodLink — A Blood Donor Connection Platform
Effective Date: September 2026  •  Last Updated: September 2026

Thinkbig Infotech (India) Pvt Ltd ("we," "us," "our") operates BloodLink (the "App"), a platform that connects blood donors with people who need blood, based on blood group and location. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the rights and choices available to you.

BloodLink is a connecting platform only. We are not a hospital, blood bank, or medical institution, and we do not provide medical advice.

1Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

DataWhy we collect it
Full nameTo identify you to other users during a match
Email addressAccount login and communication
Phone numberTo allow matched donors/requesters to contact each other
PasswordAccount security (stored encrypted, never in plain text)
AgeTo confirm blood donation eligibility (18–50)
GenderDisplayed as part of your donor profile
Blood groupCore matching criteria
City / locationTo match donors and requests within the same area
Blood request detailsPatient name, hospital, units needed, deadline — to notify nearby donors

Information collected automatically

DataWhy we collect it
Push notification tokenTo send you alerts about nearby blood requests
Basic device/technical dataApp version, OS — for debugging and reliability

We do not collect precise GPS coordinates — only the city you provide during registration.

2How We Use Your Information

  • Match blood donors with nearby blood requests
  • Send push notifications about urgent blood needs in your city
  • Allow other users to view your name, city, and blood group when browsing donors/requests
  • Allow another user to contact you by phone once you are a matched donor
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Respond to support requests
  • Maintain, debug, and improve the App

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3How Information Is Shared

With other users of the App

  • Your name, city, and blood group are visible to other logged-in users browsing donors or blood requests.
  • Your phone number is shared only at the point another user chooses to contact you, not in bulk listings.
  • Your email is not shared with other users.

We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or for marketing purposes.

Legal disclosure

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Thinkbig, our users, or the public.

4Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, blood requests, and related notifications from our active systems. Backups may persist for a limited period for disaster-recovery purposes before being purged.

5Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Phone numbers are not exposed in bulk API responses; they are revealed individually only when a user initiates contact.
  • Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel.

We work hard to protect your data, though as with any online service, we encourage you to keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

6Your Rights and Choices

You can, at any time, directly within the App:

  • View and edit your profile information (Profile → Edit)
  • Delete your account and all associated data (Profile → Delete Account)
  • Control notification permissions via your device settings

If you have questions about your data or wish to make a request outside the app, contact us using the details in Section 9.

7Children's Privacy

BloodLink is intended for users 18 years and older, consistent with standard blood donation eligibility. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18. If we learn a user is under 18, we will delete their account and associated data.

8Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will update the "Last Updated" date above when changes are made. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
